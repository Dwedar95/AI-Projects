# Refund policy (fictional example company)

## Refund window

Customers can request a refund within 30 days after delivery of the order. Requests after 30 days are declined and the customer is offered a repair or a voucher instead.

## Order status

A refund is only possible for orders with the status delivered. Orders that are still in transit must be cancelled through the delivery status process.

## Refund approval

Refunds up to 100 USD are issued by the service agent without further approval. Refunds above 100 USD need approval by the team lead of customer care. Refunds above 500 USD are decided by the team lead in a manual review.

## Refund payment

The refund is paid to the original payment method within five working days after it was issued.
