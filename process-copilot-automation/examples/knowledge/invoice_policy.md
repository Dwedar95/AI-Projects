# Accounts payable policy (fictional example company)

## Matching tolerance

An invoice is matched against its purchase order. A difference of up to 2 percent of the purchase order amount is accepted as tolerance and the invoice is released for payment.

## Payment release

Payments above 5000 USD need approval by the finance controller before the invoice is released.

## Price variance

For a price variance the accounts payable clerk asks procurement to confirm the agreed contract price with the supplier. The invoice stays blocked until a credit note or a corrected invoice is received.

## Quantity mismatch

For a quantity mismatch the invoiced quantity is compared with the goods receipt. The warehouse confirms the received quantity and the supplier is asked for a corrected invoice.

## Missing purchase order

An invoice without a purchase order reference is returned to the requesting department. The department creates a purchase order after the fact, which needs approval by the cost centre owner.
