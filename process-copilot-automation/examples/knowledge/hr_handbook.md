# Onboarding handbook (fictional example company)

## IT equipment

New employees get a laptop and a monitor on the first working day. VPN access and the email account are activated by the IT service desk before the start date. Password resets for the email account are done by the IT service desk.

## Vacation

New employees have 28 vacation days per year, pro rata in the first year. Vacation is requested in the HR portal and approved by the line manager.

## Payroll

The payroll date is the 27th of each month and employees get paid by bank transfer. The payslip is available in the HR portal on the payroll date.

## Benefits

Employees enrol in the pension plan and the health insurance benefits in the HR portal within the first 30 days. The company pays half of the pension contribution.
