# Office Facilities Guide

## Access
The office is open from 06:30 to 21:00 on working days. Access outside these hours needs a request to facility management one day ahead. Badges must be worn visibly.

## Meeting rooms and desks
Meeting rooms are booked in the calendar. A room is released automatically if nobody checks in within 10 minutes of the start time. Desks in the flexible zone are booked through the desk app up to 14 days in advance.

## Visitors and parking
Visitors are registered at reception at least one day before the visit and are accompanied at all times. The car park has 40 spaces including 6 charging points for electric vehicles. Bicycles are parked in the basement.
