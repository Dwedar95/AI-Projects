# Access Reviews and Joiner-Mover-Leaver Process

## Granting access
Access to systems follows the need-to-know principle. Requests are approved by the line manager and by the system owner. Access for external staff ends automatically after 90 days unless renewed.

## Periodic review
System owners review all access rights every 6 months. Privileged accounts are reviewed every quarter. Rights that are not confirmed within 14 days are removed.

## Leavers and movers
Accounts of leavers are disabled at 18:00 on the last working day. When an employee changes department, old rights are removed within 5 working days.
