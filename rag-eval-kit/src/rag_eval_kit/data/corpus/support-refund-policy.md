# Returns and Refund Policy

## Right of return
Unused hardware in its original packaging can be returned within 30 days of delivery. The customer pays for return shipping unless the wrong item was delivered.

## Refunds
Refunds are paid to the original payment method within 14 days after the goods arrive at the warehouse. A restocking fee of 15 percent applies to opened packaging.

## Subscriptions
Annual software subscriptions can be cancelled within 14 days of purchase for a full refund. After that, cancellation takes effect at the end of the term and no partial refund is given. Custom-configured devices cannot be returned.
