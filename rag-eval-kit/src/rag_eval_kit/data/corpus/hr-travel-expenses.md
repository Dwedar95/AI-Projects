# Travel and Expense Policy

## Booking
Business trips are booked through the travel portal. Rail is the default for journeys under 5 hours. Flights are economy class. Business class is allowed only for flights longer than 8 hours with approval from a vice president.

## Allowances
The hotel limit is 150 euros per night including breakfast. The daily meal allowance is 28 euros for a full day and 14 euros for a day of arrival or departure. Private car use is reimbursed at 0.30 euros per kilometre.

## Submitting claims
Expense claims with receipts are submitted within 30 days after the trip ends. Claims are reimbursed with the next payroll run. Claims older than 90 days are rejected.
