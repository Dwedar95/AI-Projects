# MW-200 Warranty Terms

## Coverage
The MW-200 comes with a 36 month warranty from the date of delivery. The warranty covers defects in material and workmanship. An extension to 60 months can be purchased within the first year.

## Exclusions
Damage caused by overvoltage, lightning, incorrect wiring, opened housings or operation outside the specified temperature range is not covered. Batteries are wear parts and are excluded.

## Claim process
Request a return number through the customer portal before sending a device. Advance replacement units are shipped within 2 working days for customers with a premium support contract. Repairs take about 15 working days.
