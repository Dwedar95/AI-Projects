# Support Escalation Procedure

## Levels
First-level support records the case and solves known problems. Second-level support consists of product specialists. Third level is the engineering team.

## Time limits
A case moves from first to second level if it is not solved within 2 hours for priority 1 or within 2 working days for other priorities. The duty manager is informed about every priority 1 case that is open for more than 4 hours.

## Customer escalation
A customer can ask for an escalation at any time by giving the case number to the duty manager. The duty manager calls back within 30 minutes and names a single owner for the case.
