# Laptop and Mobile Device Security

## Encryption
Every company laptop has full disk encryption enabled before handover. Recovery keys are stored in the device management system. USB storage devices are blocked unless they are hardware-encrypted models issued by IT.

## Screen lock
Devices lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity. Employees lock the screen manually whenever they leave the desk.

## Loss or theft
A lost or stolen device is reported to the service desk within 1 hour of discovery, at any time of day. The device is then wiped remotely. A police report is needed for theft outside company premises.
