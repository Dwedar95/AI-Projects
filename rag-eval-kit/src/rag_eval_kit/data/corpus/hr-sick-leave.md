# Sick Leave Policy

## Reporting an illness
Employees who are unable to work because of illness inform their line manager before 09:00 on the first day of absence, by phone or chat message. A message to a colleague is not sufficient.

## Medical certificate
A medical certificate is required from the fourth calendar day of illness. The certificate is uploaded to the HR portal. Managers may ask for a certificate from the first day in justified cases.

## Pay during illness
Salary continues to be paid in full for up to 6 weeks of the same illness. After that period the statutory health insurance pays sickness benefit.
