# Annual Leave Policy

## Entitlement
Full-time employees receive 30 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Part-time employees receive a pro-rata share based on their weekly working days. New joiners accrue 2.5 days for each full month of employment in their first year.

## Requesting leave
Leave requests are submitted in the HR portal at least 14 calendar days before the first day of absence. The line manager approves or declines within 5 working days. Requests longer than 15 consecutive working days also need approval from the department head.

## Carry-over
Up to 10 unused days may be carried over into the next year. Carried-over days expire on 31 March. Leave is not paid out except when employment ends.
