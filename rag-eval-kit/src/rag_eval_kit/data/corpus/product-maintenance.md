# MW-200 Maintenance Schedule

## Routine checks
Inspect the device every 12 months. Remove dust from the ventilation slots with dry compressed air. Check that the terminal screws are tightened to 0.5 newton metres.

## Battery
The real-time clock is buffered by a CR2032 coin cell. Replace the battery every 5 years or when the web interface shows a clock warning. Settings are not lost when the battery is changed while the device is powered.

## Spare parts and disposal
Only the battery and the terminal blocks are replaceable in the field. Defective devices are returned to the manufacturer. Do not dispose of the gateway with household waste.
