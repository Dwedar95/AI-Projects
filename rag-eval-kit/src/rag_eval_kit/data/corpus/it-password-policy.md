# Password Policy

## Requirements
Passwords have at least 14 characters. Passphrases made of several words are recommended. A password must not contain the user name or the company name and must differ from the last 10 passwords.

## Rotation
Passwords are not rotated on a schedule. A change is forced only when there is evidence of compromise, for example a match in a breach database.

## Lockout
After 8 failed sign-in attempts the account is locked for 30 minutes. The service desk can unlock an account after verifying the caller with a callback to the registered phone number. Password managers approved by IT are provided to every employee.
