# Data Retention Schedule

## Business records
Invoices and accounting records are retained for 10 years. Contracts are retained for 6 years after they end. Project documentation is retained for 5 years after project closure.

## Personal data
Application documents of rejected candidates are deleted after 6 months. Personnel files are deleted 3 years after the employee leaves, except payroll data, which follows the 10 year rule.

## Communication data
Chat messages are deleted after 2 years. Mailboxes of former employees are deleted 90 days after the last working day. A legal hold issued by the legal department suspends deletion.
