# Procurement Guideline

## Purchase requests
Every purchase above 1000 euros needs a purchase order before the order is placed. Three quotes are required for purchases above 10000 euros.

## Approval limits
Team leads approve up to 5000 euros, department heads up to 25000 euros and the managing director anything above. Contracts with a term longer than 24 months are reviewed by the legal department.

## Suppliers
New suppliers complete a questionnaire on information security and sustainability before the first order. Suppliers that process personal data sign a data processing agreement. Payment terms are 30 days net unless agreed otherwise.
