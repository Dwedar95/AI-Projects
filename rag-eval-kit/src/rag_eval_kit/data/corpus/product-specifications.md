# MW-200 Technical Specifications

## Hardware
The gateway has a quad-core ARM processor, 2 GB of RAM and 16 GB of flash storage. Power consumption is typically 6 watts and at most 10 watts.

## Interfaces
Two Gigabit Ethernet ports, one RS-485 port, one RS-232 port, two USB 2.0 ports and four digital inputs. An optional LTE module can be fitted in the expansion slot.

## Protocols and approvals
Supported protocols are Modbus RTU, Modbus TCP, OPC UA and MQTT. The housing has protection class IP20. The device carries the CE mark and weighs 310 grams.
