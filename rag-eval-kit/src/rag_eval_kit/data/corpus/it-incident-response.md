# Security Incident Response

## Reporting
Every employee reports a suspected security incident immediately to the security hotline or the incident mailbox. Examples are phishing messages that were clicked, malware warnings and misdirected emails with personal data.

## Severity levels
Severity 1 is an ongoing attack or confirmed data breach and is handled around the clock with a first response within 15 minutes. Severity 2 gets a response within 4 hours, severity 3 within one working day.

## Notification duties
A personal data breach is reported to the data protection authority within 72 hours after the company becomes aware of it. The data protection officer decides on the notification. A post-incident review is held within 10 working days.
