# Billing and Invoicing

## Invoices
Invoices are issued on the first working day of each month and sent as PDF to the billing contact. The payment term is 30 days net. Each invoice shows the purchase order number supplied by the customer.

## Late payment
A reminder is sent 7 days after the due date. Interest of 9 percentage points above the base rate is charged from day 31. Support is suspended when an invoice is more than 60 days overdue.

## Changes
The billing address or contact can be changed in the customer portal. Changes apply from the next invoice. Credit notes are issued within 5 working days after approval.
