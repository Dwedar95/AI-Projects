# MW-200 Edge Gateway Installation Guide

## Mounting
The MW-200 is mounted on a 35 mm DIN rail inside a control cabinet. Keep 50 mm of free space above and below the housing for air flow. The permitted ambient temperature is minus 20 to plus 60 degrees Celsius.

## Power and wiring
Connect a 24 V DC supply to terminals 1 and 2. The supply must be protected with a 2 A fuse. Use shielded cable for the RS-485 port and ground the shield at one end only.

## First start
After power-up the status LED blinks green for about 90 seconds while the device boots. Open the web interface at the address printed on the label and set a new administrator password before any other configuration.
