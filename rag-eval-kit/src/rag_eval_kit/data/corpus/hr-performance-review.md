# Performance Review Process

## Cycle
Performance reviews take place twice a year, in June and in December. Goals are agreed in January and recorded in the HR portal.

## Rating
Managers rate each goal on a scale from 1 to 5. The overall rating is calibrated in a meeting of all managers of the department. Employees see their rating before salary decisions are communicated.

## Salary adjustment
Salary adjustments take effect on 1 April. A rating of 4 or 5 qualifies for the merit increase pool. Employees can appeal a rating to HR within 14 days.
