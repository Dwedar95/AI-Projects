# Onboarding Guide for New Employees

## Before the first day
New employees receive their contract, a welcome email and the IT order form two weeks before their start date. The laptop is prepared by the service desk and handed over on the first morning.

## First week
Day one starts at 09:30 at the reception with the badge photo. The buddy, a colleague from the same team, guides the newcomer through the first 4 weeks. Mandatory courses on information security and workplace safety are completed within the first 5 working days.

## Probation
The probation period is 6 months. A feedback conversation takes place after 3 months and a final review two weeks before probation ends.
