# Data Classification Standard

## Levels
Information is classified as public, internal, confidential or strictly confidential. The owner of the information assigns the level when the document is created.

## Handling rules
Confidential information is shared only with named people and is encrypted when sent outside the company. Strictly confidential information, such as merger plans and health data, must not be stored on mobile devices and must not be entered into external AI tools.

## Labelling
Office documents and emails carry a sensitivity label. Unlabelled documents are treated as internal. Printing strictly confidential documents requires the secure print function with badge release.
