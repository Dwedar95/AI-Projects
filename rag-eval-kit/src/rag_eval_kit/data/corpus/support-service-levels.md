# Customer Support Service Levels

## Support plans
Three plans are offered. Basic includes email support on working days from 08:00 to 17:00. Standard adds phone support. Premium includes support around the clock and a named technical account manager.

## Response times
For a priority 1 case, which means production is down, the first response comes within 1 hour on Premium, 4 hours on Standard and the next working day on Basic. Priority 3 questions are answered within 3 working days on every plan.

## Service credits
If a response time is missed, Premium customers receive a service credit of 5 percent of the monthly fee per missed case, up to 25 percent per month.
