# Parental Leave Policy

## Eligibility
Every employee with a child under the age of eight can take parental leave, regardless of length of service. Both parents can take leave at the same time.

## Notice period
Parental leave is announced in writing at least 7 weeks before it starts. The announcement states the start and end date. HR confirms within 10 working days.

## Returning to work
Employees return to their previous role or an equivalent one. A phased return with reduced hours can be agreed for up to 6 months. The company tops up the statutory parental allowance to 80 percent of net salary for the first 3 months.
