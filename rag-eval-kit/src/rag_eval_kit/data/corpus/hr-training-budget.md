# Training and Development Budget

## Annual budget
Each employee has a personal learning budget of 1500 euros per calendar year. The budget covers courses, conferences, books and certification exam fees. Unused budget does not roll over.

## Approval
Spending up to 500 euros is approved by the line manager. Higher amounts also need the department head. Requests are raised in the learning portal before booking.

## Learning time
Employees can use up to 5 working days per year for training. Certification exams passed on the first attempt are rewarded with a bonus of 300 euros. If an employee leaves within 12 months after a course that cost more than 3000 euros, half of the cost is repaid.
