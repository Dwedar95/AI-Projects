# Remote Work Policy

## Scope
Employees whose role allows it can work remotely up to 3 days per week. Tuesdays are team days: everyone is expected in the office unless on leave or on a business trip.

## Working from abroad
Working from another country is limited to 20 working days per year and needs approval from HR and the tax team at least 4 weeks in advance. Countries outside the European Economic Area are not permitted.

## Equipment
The company provides a laptop, a headset and one external monitor for the home office. A one-time allowance of 250 euros is available for a desk chair. Private devices must not be used for company data.
