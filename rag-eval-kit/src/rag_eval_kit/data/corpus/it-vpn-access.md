# VPN Access

## Who needs it
The VPN is required to reach internal file shares, the build servers and the ERP system from outside the office. Cloud applications such as email and chat work without it.

## Setup
The VPN client is installed automatically on managed laptops. Sign-in uses the company account and a second factor. Sessions end after 12 hours and must be re-established.

## Restrictions
Split tunnelling is disabled, so all traffic passes through the company gateway while connected. Connections from privately owned devices are blocked. Connection problems are reported to the service desk with the error message shown by the client.
